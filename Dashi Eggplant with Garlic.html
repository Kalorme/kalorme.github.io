Dashi Eggplant with Garlic

Koen2 servings5 min10 min15 min

A delicious Asian stir-fry, this dish gets flavour from eggplant fried with garlic and dashi.

  • 600 g eggplant 2–3 medium
  • 20 g garlic 4–5 cloves
  • 20 ml vegetable oil
  • 2 g chili flakes
  • 3 g salt
  • 1 g black pepper
  • 5 g dashi powder
  1. Cut the eggplant into chunks of about 3–4 cm. Peel and finely mince the garlic.
  2. 2. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the eggplant and cook for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and starting to brown.
  3. 3. Add the minced garlic and cook for another 2–3 minutes, stirring frequently, until the garlic becomes a fragrant paste and blends with the eggplant.
  4. 4. Stir in the chili flakes, salt, pepper, and dashi powder. Cook for 1–2 more minutes to let the flavors combine, then serve warm.
