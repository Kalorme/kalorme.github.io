Chicken and Parsley Egg-soup

kruiger.nl

Egg soup with chicken and parsley. A soup that’s ready in under 20 minutes. Easy for a quick bite, and with some rice, it becomes a full meal.

  • 30 ml water
  • 6 g cornstarch
  • 750 ml chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 eggs about 100 g
  • 10 ml ketjap manis
  • 150 g chicken breast cut into small pieces
  • 1 clove garlic about 5 g
  • 1 g salt
  • 0.5 g pepper
  • 5 g fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • 10 ml oil for frying
  1. Cut the chicken into small strips or pieces (this helps it cook faster). Fry it with the finely chopped garlic in oil until cooked through and golden.
  2. Once done, turn off the heat and set it aside. Bring the broth to a boil and, in the meantime, mix the water and cornstarch in a small bowl.
  3. When the broth starts boiling, stir in the cornstarch mixture.
  4. Beat the eggs and slowly pour them into the pot while gently stirring continuously. Once the eggs have set, lower the heat and add the chicken and garlic to the egg soup.
  5. Add the finely chopped parsley. Divide the soup between two bowls.
