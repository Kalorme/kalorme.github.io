Loco Moco Sauce

Koen45 min25 min

Variant of the Hawaiian brown gravy from the popular dish known as Loco Moco. This simple gravy is full of rich flavor and is quick and simple to make. Ala Koen

  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Onions half caramelized
  • Garlic
  • Mushrooms finely chopped
  • Chicken stock
  • Soy sauce
  • Rice vinegar small amount
  • Shaoxin wine
  • Msg
  • Pepper
  1. Start by cooking the chopped mushrooms, set aside
  2. Add some oil in the same pan, cook onions until half caramelised. Add garlic halfway through. Add Shaoxin wine.
  3. Add flour and butter, then add the chicken stock, stir until you have a thick roux.
  4. Add mushrooms back, add soy sauce, vinegar etc..
