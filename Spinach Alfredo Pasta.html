Spinach Alfredo Pasta

Koen45 min20 min25 min

Creamy and rich, yet never heavy, this Spinach Alfredo Pasta is an easy and satisfying comfort food.

  • 340 g pasta
  • 50 g butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 4 shallots finely chopped
  • 250 ml heavy cream
  • 75 g grated Parmesan or any other hard Italian cheese
  • 1 g salt
  • Freshly cracked black pepper to taste
  • 450 g cream spinach
  • Olive oil
  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil to cook the pasta. Once boiling, add the pasta and cook until al dente. Drain the pasta in a colander.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, prepare the sauce. Add olive oil, shallots and minced garlic to a deep skillet or pot and sauté over medium heat for 1-2 minutes, or just until the shallots are soft and fragrant.
  3. Turn the heat down to medium-low. Add the cream to the skillet along with the butter (cut into chunks). Stir and mix the butter in the cream.
  4. Whisk in the grated Parmesan cheese and continue to whisk as the sauce comes up to a gentle simmer. When it reaches a simmer, the sauce will thicken. Turn the heat down to low to keep the sauce warm. Season the sauce with salt and a generous amount of freshly cracked pepper.
  5. Add the spinach to the sauce and stir it.
  6. Finally, add the cooked and drained pasta to the spinach Alfredo sauce and stir to combine. Serve immediately.
