Crispy Skin Salmon

Koen410 min10 min

The crispiest salmon skin basted in garlic sage butter!

  • 720g salmon fillets, skin-on 4 fillets, 180g each
  • 30ml oil
  • 5g salt
  • 1g black pepper
  • 90g butter
  • 12g garlic crushed
  • 5ml lemon juice, plus more to taste
  • 4g fresh sage leaves optional
  • lemon wedges, for serving
  1. Pat the salmon skin dry very well with paper towels, rub salt on skin. For extra crispiness, place the fillets skin side up in the fridge uncovered for 1 hour if time allows.
  2. Just before cooking, season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Heat half the oil in a pan using baking paper over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place the salmon skin side down.
  4. Use a spatula to press each fillet down gently for about 10 seconds to ensure the skin stays flat as it cooks.
  5. Cook skin side down for about 3-5 minutes, until the salmon is about half cooked and the skin is crispy.
  6. Add the butter to the pan. When the butter is fully melted and foaming, add the garlic and sage leaves if using.
  7. Tilt the pan slightly and begin basting the salmon with the bubbling butter for 90 seconds, spooning the butter over the fillets continuously.
  8. Remove from heat, add lemon juice to the butter, and let the salmon rest for 3 minutes. The internal temperature should rise to about 53°C.
  9. Serve the salmon skin side up. Spoon some of the garlic butter over the top and garnish with parsley and lemon wedges.
