Thoum and Zhoug Chicken Wrap

youtube.com5 min20 min25 min

This recipe features tender, marinated chicken leg with crispy skin, cooked on the stovetop and finished with a touch of real wood smoke. It’s served with a quick garlic-labneh sauce and a fresh, spicy cilantro-based zhoug. A straightforward, flavorful meal ready in under 25 minutes.

Shawarma Chicken

  • 1 deboned chicken leg, skin on 250 g
  • Pinch salt
  • Small pinch MSG
  • 5 g baharat or falafel seasoning mix -&gt; check notes to make yourself
  • 5 g curry powder
  • 5 g smoked paprika
  • 15 ml neutral oil

Thoum

  • 200 g labneh or dense yogurt
  • 30 g mayonnaise
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated about 5 g
  • 3 g granulated or freeze-dried garlic
  • Zest of 1 lime
  • 15 ml lime juice

Zhoug

  • 80 g coriander stems included
  • 4 green chilies 25 g
  • 1 garlic clove about 5 g
  • 2.5 g salt
  • 2 g ground cardamom
  • 2 g ground cumin
  • 2 g curry powder
  • 50 g neutral oil
  • 30 ml vinegar
  • 30 ml olive oil
  • 5–10 g red pepper flakes optional
  1. Season deboned chicken leg with salt and a pinch of MSG on both sides. Mix falafel spice (or Arabic 7-spice), curry powder, smoked paprika, and oil. Brush onto the flesh side only and refrigerate to marinate.
  2. For the thoum, mix labneh, a bit of mayo, grated garlic, crushed freeze-dried garlic, lime zest, a squeeze of lime juice, salt, and a tiny bit of MSG. Stir and set aside.
  3. For zhoug, blend coriander (use stems), green chilies, a garlic clove, salt, cumin, cardamom, curry powder, and neutral oil. Add vinegar if needed to loosen. Stir in olive oil after blending.
  4. Cook chicken skin side down in a cold non-stick pan over medium heat. Press with baking paper and a heavy object. Cook ~5 minutes until skin is crispy, flip, press again, and cook another ~5 minutes.
  5. For extra smoky flavor, heat a small piece of wood or charcoal until glowing. Drop into an oiled, foil-lined ramekin, cover to trap smoke for a few minutes.
  6. Assemble your wrap
