Arayes

recipetineats.com10 pita halves15 min15 min30 min

Lebanese street food - pita bread stuffed with spiced kofta meat, pan fried until crispy! The unique method here is that it's cooked with raw meat inside, so the tasty meat juices soaks into and flavours the pita bread as it cooks.

For the pita and meat filling:

  • 5 pita breads
  • 5 g olive oil spray or 20 g olive oil for brushing
  • 100 g brown onion about 1/2 medium
  • 500 g ground lamb or beef
  • 6 g garlic 2 cloves, finely grated
  • 5 g parsley, finely chopped optional
  • 6 g ground coriander
  • 6 g ground cumin
  • 3 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g ground allspice
  • 0.5 g cayenne pepper
  • 7 g kosher or cooking salt

For the whipped tahini yogurt sauce:

  • 240 g plain yogurt
  • 30 g tahini or Chinese sesame paste
  • 15 g lemon juice
  • 3 g garlic 1 clove, finely grated
  • 3 g kosher or cooking salt

Prepare filling

  1. Grate the onion directly into a mixing bowl to capture all the juices.
  2. Add the ground meat, garlic, parsley, coriander, cumin, paprika, allspice, cayenne, and salt. Mix thoroughly with your hands until evenly combined.
  3. Divide the mixture into 10 portions (55–60 g each) and shape each into a semi-circle patty slightly smaller than a pita half

Stuff pitas

  1. Gently open each pita half and stuff with one portion of meat.
  2. Press to spread the meat evenly inside to the edges. If your pita tears, warm it slightly before stuffing to make it more flexible.

Cook Arayes

  1. Preheat oven to 50°C (to keep Arayes warm).
  2. Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Lightly spray or brush both sides of each stuffed pita with olive oil.
  3. Cook 2–3 pieces at a time for 2 minutes per side, pressing down gently with a spatula, until golden and crisp.
  4. Transfer cooked Arayes to a tray with a rack and place in the oven. Repeat with remaining pieces.

Make tahini sauce & serve

  1. In a heatproof bowl, combine yogurt, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, and salt.
  2. Microwave for 15 seconds, then whisk until smooth and lightly whipped.
  3. Serve warm or at room temperature alongside hot Arayes, cut in halves if desired.
