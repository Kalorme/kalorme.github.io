Mì Xào Giòn

takestwoeggs.com3 servings20 min15 min35 min

This mì xào giòn or Crispy Noodle Stir Fry is made with fresh seafood, chicken and a colorful variety of vegetables. Nothing beats that ultra crispy golden noodle nest that is glazed in a rich garlicy soy sauce.

Sauce

  • 60 ml oyster sauce
  • 60 ml light soy sauce
  • 60 ml dark soy sauce
  • 50 g granulated sugar
  • 2 g white pepper
  • 5 ml Shaoxing wine
  • 5 ml sesame oil
  • 415 ml chicken broth
  • 16 g cornstarch
  • 75 ml water

Crispy Noodles

  • 425 g fresh thin egg noodles wonton or chow mein noodles
  • 2.4 liters vegetable or canola oil for deep-frying

Stir Fry

  • 280 g chicken thigh, thinly sliced
  • 140 g squid, cleaned and sliced optional
  • 140 g shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 15 ml vegetable oil
  • 60 g yellow onion ½ medium, thin wedge cut
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced about 18 g
  • 2 shallots, minced about 30 g
  • 1 carrot, peeled and sliced about 100 g
  • 8 shiitake mushrooms, stems removed and sliced about 120 g
  • 8 baby bok choy, stems and leaves separated about 250 g total
  • 2 stalks green onion about 30 g

Sauce and Marinade

  1. Mix the sauce: In a small mixing bowl, combine 60 ml oyster sauce, 60 ml light soy sauce, 60 ml dark soy sauce, 50 g sugar, 2 g white pepper, 5 ml Shaoxing wine, and 5 ml sesame oil.
  2. Marinate the protein: Season 280 g sliced chicken, 140 g cleaned shrimp, and 140 g sliced squid with salt and pepper. Add 30 ml (2 tablespoons) of the sauce mixture and mix. Marinate for 10 minutes.

Crispy Noodles

  1. Prepare the wok: Heat a large wok or deep pot with 10–12 cm of vegetable or peanut oil to 190°C.
  2. Fry the noodles: Working in batches, separate 425 g fresh thin egg noodles and shape into nests on a spider strainer. Submerge in the oil for 10–20 seconds, or until puffed and crispy. (Optional: For a pronounced bird’s nest shape, press another smaller strainer on top while frying.) Transfer to a wire rack to drain and cool.

Stir-Fry

  1. Cook aromatics: In a large wok or pan, heat 15 ml vegetable oil on high. Stir-fry 60 g onion, 18 g minced garlic (about 6 cloves), and 30 g minced shallot until browned and fragrant.
  2. Cook the protein: Add marinated chicken and stir-fry for 1 minute. Then add squid and stir-fry for 30 seconds. Finally, add shrimp and stir-fry until pink.
  3. Add chicken broth: Pour in 415 ml chicken broth and bring to a boil on high heat.
  4. Add vegetables and sauce: When boiling, add 100 g sliced carrot and toss for 1 minute.
  5. Add 120 g sliced shiitake mushrooms, toss another 1 minute. Slowly stir in the slurry (16 g cornstarch + 75 ml water), 1 tablespoon at a time, stirring for 30 seconds between each addition.
  6. Add 60–75 ml of the remaining sauce mixture in stages, tasting after each addition to adjust concentration.
  7. Add 250 g baby bok choy (stems and leaves separated) and 30 g green onion.
  8. Stir-fry until vegetables are softened, about 1–2 minutes.

Assembly

  1. Place crispy noodle nests on a large plate. Top with stir-fried vegetables and protein. Spoon over extra sauce and serve immediately.

Prep all the ingredients before you start cooking. If you plan out the recipe ahead of time by making the sauce, the cornstarch slurry, prepping the vegetables, and marinating the proteins in separate bowls it will speed up and simplify the cooking process.

Fry the noodles at 190°C. You do not want overly soggy noodles if the temperature is too low or burnt noodles if the oil is too hot.

Pour the cornstarch 1 tablespoon at a time and mix. This allows the cornstarch to be evenly distributed in the chicken broth and thicken up the sauce. Pouring it all at once may result in one section of the broth that is coagulated and the rest overly watery.

Use enough sauce to taste. This recipe will make an excess of sauce. DO NOT USE ALL THE SAUCE. This will yield an overly salty sauce. Make sure to add the sauce 1 tablespoon at a time and toss to reach your desired saltiness. I used about 5 tablespoons of the sauce in this recipe.

Storage Instructions:you can store the stir fry apart from the noodles in an airtight container for up to 5 days in the refrigerator. The noodles will lose their crispiness within 24 hours and turn stale. I recommend freshly frying up the noodles when you want to serve.
