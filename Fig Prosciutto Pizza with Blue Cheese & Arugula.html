Fig Prosciutto Pizza with Blue Cheese & Arugula

katykeck.com10 min20 min30 min

This Fig Prosciutto Pizza is a far cry from those gummy Newtons you grew up on. Sweet jammy fig butter, with crispy and salty Prosciutto, creamy rich Cambozola and fresh luscious figs let this pizza span a meal from appetizer to dessert.

  • 450 g fresh pizza dough
  • 310 g fig butter or fig jam
  • Oil for drizzling e.g. lemon oil or neutral oil for baking; truffle oil after baking, optional
  • 8 fresh figs (about 150 g), sliced
  • 225 g Cambozola cheese or creamy blue cheese like Gorgonzola
  • 8 slices prosciutto approx. 120 g
  • 90 g arugula
  • Crema di Balsamico or balsamic reduction to taste
  • Flaky sea salt (e.g. Maldon), to finish
  1. Preheat oven and pizza stone to 220°C. Check pizza dough directions to confirm this is best for your dough.
  2. Lightly flour a work surface and stretch or roll the pizza dough into a 30 cm diameter round. If you are using the roll-out dough that comes in a popping-fresh canister, follow those directions and roll into a rectangle, but plan on using more topping ingredients. Transfer the dough to a lightly oiled pan.
  3. Spread the fig butter to create a thin layer, leaving a 1 cm border of dough around the outside. Brush the exposed outer ring of dough with a favorite oil.
  4. Set the pan on the pizza stone and bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is set.
  5. Carefully remove from oven, adding the figs, some dollops of Cambozola and the Prosciutto. I like to take a half-slice of Prosciutto and twirl into a rosette, but some prefer to chop it and distribute the flavor more uniformly. Maybe you should be making two pizzas?
  6. Return to oven and bake for 10 more minutes or until the crust is golden and the Prosciutto starts to crisp. The cheese should be melty.
  7. Remove from oven and top with the arugula. Drizzle with a balsamic reduction (or Crema di Balsamico) and sprinkle with a flaky finishing salt.
